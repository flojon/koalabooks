# Guide: Migrating Opening Balances to a New Chart of Accounts

Switching to a new chart of accounts (kontoplan) is a significant change. To ensure your accounting remains accurate, you must transfer the opening balances from your old accounts to the appropriate new accounts. Follow these steps:

## Step-by-Step Migration Guide

1. **Export Current Balances**
   - Before switching, export a trial balance (balansrapport) as of the last day using the old chart. This report lists all account balances.

2. **Map Old Accounts to New Accounts**
   - Create a mapping table: for each old account, specify the corresponding new account. Consult your accountant if unsure.

3. **Set Up the New Chart of Accounts**
   - Import or create the new chart in the system. Double-check account numbers and names.

4. **Create an Opening Balance Journal Entry**
   - In the new fiscal year (or after switching), create a manual journal entry:
     - Debit and credit the new accounts with the opening balances from your mapping table.
     - The sum of debits and credits must match (balance).
     - Use a clear description, e.g., "Opening balance migration from old chart."

5. **Verify the Migration**
   - Run a trial balance report in the new chart. Confirm that balances match your exported report.
   - Check that all accounts are correctly mapped and no balances are missing.

6. **Retain Documentation**
   - Keep your mapping table and exported reports for audit and reference.

## Tips
- If you have many accounts, consider using spreadsheet software to prepare the mapping and calculations.
- For complex migrations, consult a professional accountant.
- Do not delete the old chart or data—retain for historical reference.

---

This guide ensures a clear, auditable process for moving balances when adopting a new chart of accounts.
