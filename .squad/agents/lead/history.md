### 2026-04-17: Feature Gaps vs. Swedish Bookkeeping Systems
- Identified key missing features compared to established Swedish systems: integrated bank feeds, payroll, advanced VAT automation, NE-bilaga support, project accounting, multi-user roles, API/integrations, deep automation, onboarding, migration tools, real-time support, and core lifecycle features (in progress).
- These gaps highlight areas for future investment and competitive parity.
- Documented in team decisions for roadmap alignment.
